## Dissenting view
Chloe Pascoe (PPC Hero, 2020-07-09, https://ppchero.com/preventing-and-navigating-google-merchant-center-suspensions/) puts "contact Google Support chat and your account rep" as step one for a suspension and lists compliance fixes. Williams' tax post starts from the notification's flagged products and the account's own settings before anyone at Google is involved. The disagreement is about where diagnosis starts — Google's explanation or your data; the canon starts with the data. (Pascoe is not canon; cited as dissent only.)

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- Automatic item updates (price, sale price, availability, condition) are on by default; they read schema.org markup or fall back to extractors; on a detected mismatch the product may be *disapproved* rather than updated, especially where values change more than once a day; updates may stop if mismatches become too numerous; multiple strikethrough prices confuse the price extractor. [Google docs, undated]
- Misrepresentation: most account-level violations get a warning email with 7 or 28 calendar days to fix; egregious categories (unacceptable business practices, coordinated deception, dishonest pricing) are suspended on detection with no warning and rarely reinstated; reviews typically take 7 business days; bulk appeals with violating offers still present can fail immediately and limit future appeals. [Google docs, undated]
- Incorrect GTIN/brand/MPN → disapproval; missing an available identifier → limited visibility. [Google docs, undated]
- Product Diagnostics and Promotions views appeared inside Google Ads for linked accounts in mid-2026 (Vlietstra/ZATO, 2026-06-15).
- Feed rules renamed attribute rules; supplemental sources behind the Advanced data source management add-on (see dossier 65). [Google docs]

## Baseline check
Read `google-ads-audit-ecommerce` (Layer 2: "Disapproved products — fix immediately per GMC error reason"; GTIN, price mismatch rows). The baseline says look at Diagnostics and fix. This skill adds the weekly/daily detection logic (clicks-weighted disapproval alert, Shopping-serving check), the pattern → cause map practitioners use, the suspension timers and what to do inside them, and when to change an `id` or exclude a destination instead of "fixing".

## Judgment calls
- Three of the ZATO sources are by Williams' colleagues (Talhelm, Vlietstra); counted under ZATO as Tier 1 outlet but noted as not Williams himself.
- Rooijmans' ">10%" is a client observation, not a study; the skill cites it as such.
- The row says "Weekly"; Rooijmans runs detection daily. The skill sets detection daily by script and the pattern review weekly.
- Could not fetch Google's "Diagnostics" help page; the platform section relies on the policy, UPI and automatic-updates pages that did load.

## Canon strength: Adequate
Two independent Tier 1 practitioners (Rooijmans with scripts; Williams/ZATO with case-based procedures) plus Google policy timers; no cross-account disapproval statistics.

## Invalidated by
Google changing the misrepresentation warning windows (7/28 days) or review time, removing the Content API product-status endpoint the scripts use, or moving diagnostics wholly into Google Ads with different issue taxonomies.
