# Dossier — 87 Location options: presence vs. presence-or-interest

## Sources

1. **Source** Brad Geddes, Adalysis blog, "Understanding & Optimizing Google Location Targeting Settings", https://adalysis.com/blog/understanding-optimizing-google-location-targeting-settings/ · **Tier** 1 · **Date** 2021-09-01 (page modified 2026-05-11) · **Claim** Default lets anyone in the location *or searching about it* see ads, so a country target reaches people abroad. Decision rule: local service businesses → Presence; national/online sellers → Presence-or-interest is often fine; national companies that serve everywhere → Presence with the country selected. If budgets are not being hit (i.e. budget-constrained), remove the interest-based group and target Presence only. Google has no UI report by *user* location; aggregate via API/pivot and compare cost per conversion by location. Under tCPA/tROAS there are no location bid modifiers, only on/off. · **Evidence or opinion** One example account (video transcript): ~$16 cost per conversion for people inside the targeted location vs ~$1,000 for people outside it. Single-account anecdote with numbers.

2. **Source** Nils Rooijmans, "Matched location != User location", https://nilsrooijmans.com/daily/matched-location-user-location · **Tier** 1 · **Date** 2024-08-12 (modified 2024-09-16) · **Claim** The "Matched locations" report in the UI does not show where users are; it shows the location that matched (which can be the location of interest). Example: campaign targets the Netherlands, an Amsterdam user searches "hotels in Riga" → Riga is the matched location. ## Dissenting view
Geddes (2021) is the dissent: Presence-or-interest is *often better* for national or online sellers because people travel and research from outside a region, and exclusions can carve out bad regions while keeping interest targeting. Google (Help) also claims +5% conversions on Search for Travel/Real Estate/Education advertisers who moved from Presence to Presence-or-interest (internal data, 21 May–1 Jun 2022). The disagreement is about *who the extra reach is*: Rooijmans (2024, 2026) treats out-of-area matches as waste or fraud by default; Geddes treats them as a segment to measure first and remove only when the CPA gap proves it (his own example: ~$16 vs ~$1,000).

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- [Google docs] Targeting options: "Presence or interest" (default, recommended; "most campaigns see fewer impressions when they switch away") and "Presence". Exclusion option: Presence only (default). Apply to Search, Display, Demand Gen; Hotel campaigns use Presence only. Google suggests Presence only for "sensitive verticals with strict limits" or when you must reach only people physically there. Claims +5% conversions on Search for Travel/Real Estate/Education moving Presence → Presence-or-interest (Google internal data, 5/21/2022–6/1/2022). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038
- [Google docs] Google Ads API blog, 2023-02: from 2023-04-14 `positive_geo_target_type = SEARCH_INTEREST` removed (Search, Shopping, Display) and `negative_geo_target_type = PRESENCE_OR_INTEREST` removed (PMax, Search, Shopping, Display); auto-migration of existing campaigns from 2023-04-24; `SETTING_VALUE_NOT_COMPATIBLE_WITH_CAMPAIGN` on old values. Remaining values: PRESENCE_OR_INTEREST / PRESENCE for targeting, PRESENCE for exclusion. https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2023/02/changes-to-location-targeting-in-google.html (SEJ, 2023-02-27, reported Google's rationale: "low adoption", "confusing".)
- [Google docs, via SEL] Advanced location options (Presence / Presence-or-interest) became visible for Performance Max campaigns with a Merchant Center feed (SEL report, undated, ~2022). https://searchengineland.com/google-updates-advanced-location-options-for-performance-max-campaigns-386294
- [Google docs] "Matched locations" is the default UI report; "User location" needs a Report Editor custom report (per Rooijmans 2024; Google's old "User locations" report removed).

## Baseline check
Read `google-ads-account-audit` (Layer 7c: "Is 'People in AND searching for' selected?" — one checklist line, and the phrasing is the pre-2023 option set) and `google-ads-segmentation` not read (not on topic). The baseline tells you to check the setting; it does not say which report proves leakage (matched ≠ user location), the budget-constrained rule, the CPA-gap test, the PMax exclusion path, or when interest targeting is the right call. This skill adds all of those and names the 2023 option removal.

## Judgment calls
- Position taken: Presence by default on every campaign, with Presence-or-interest as a deliberate exception (travel/hospitality, relocation, destination retail) that must be proven with a user-location CPA comparison. Reason: Rooijmans (2024, 2026) and Optmyzr (2025) agree on the default; Geddes (2021) supplies the test that decides the exception.
- The CPA-gap threshold is taken from Geddes's example (inside vs outside ≈ 60×) and generalised to "outside-area CPA worse than 2× the in-area CPA, or zero conversions on material spend" — the 2× figure is Epiminds' operational choice, flagged as such in the skill, not a source number.
- Optmyzr's "30 days before excluding" is cited as the floor, labelled as Optmyzr's unsourced advice.
- SEL Slusky (2015) and SEJ Ani (2024) excluded from claims: no evidence, not canon.

## Canon strength: Adequate
Four Tier 1 sources (Geddes; Rooijmans ×3) plus a weak Optmyzr post; only Geddes brings numbers, and from one account. No cross-account study on presence vs interest exists in the canon.

## Invalidated by
Google removing the "Presence" targeting option (as it removed "Search interest" in April 2023), re-adding a native User-location report, or adding location bid modifiers to tCPA/tROAS.
