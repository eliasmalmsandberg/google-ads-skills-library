# Dossier — 26 Combinations report & asset ratings — what they hide

Research note: WebSearch budget exhausted mid-category; sources reached by direct fetch. The index row names "asset ratings"; Google deprecated the Performance label column and replaced it with asset-level clicks/conversions in mid-2025 (below), so the skill covers both the old labels (still referenced in many audits) and the new asset statistics.

## Sources

## Dissenting view
Google Ads Help (asset reporting page) says asset-level CTR/CPC/CPA/ROAS are "directional" and advises evaluating at campaign or asset-group level, not per asset — which is close to Geddes' position. The real dissent is the baseline/common practice of pausing "Low" assets on sight (and Vallaeys' 2021 "replace Low" rule): Geddes (2022, 2026) argues a single-ad-group asset read, label or statistic, cannot tell you the asset is bad, only that it was bad in the combinations and ad group it served in. The disagreement is whether an asset-level signal is actionable on its own.

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- [Google docs] Campaign-level asset reporting for RSAs (answer/9781208): the "Performance label" column is deprecated; full performance statistics available only for dates on or after 2025-06-05; only active assets with impressions in the last 30 days appear; clicks combine headline clicks and asset clicks (segment by click type for asset-only); CTR = clicks ÷ impressions when the ad showed the asset; asset-level metrics are counted per asset instance and may not sum to totals; asset-level CTR/CPC/CPA/ROAS are indicators only because they reflect combinations served together; an asset with zero impressions for several weeks should be considered for replacement; data updates daily; "Google AI" label marks text customised by Google in the Added-by column.
- [Google docs] About RSAs (answer/7684791): the per-ad stats table totals across all combinations; the ad preview does not show every combination; the combinations view is linked but not described.
- [Google docs] Google Ads API RSA overview (2026-09-30): statistics are collected on headline/description combinations and the system favours combinations that perform well together; automatically created assets are not returned with the ad's assets.

## Baseline check
Read `google-ads-ad-copy` (audit step 1: sort assets by performance rating; pause Low; keep Best; wait on Learning). That column no longer exists, and the baseline never explains why the top-served combination is not the best one. This skill adds: what each view actually contains, the combination-serving bias toward CTR, the non-summable/directional nature of asset stats, and the replacement decision rule that requires a pinned-combination or cross-ad-group read.

## Judgment calls
- Kept Vallaeys' 2021 thresholds (5,000 top-of-page impressions/30 days; 2 weeks no impressions) as the historical label rule and the only hard numbers available; marked the 5,000 figure as applying to the deprecated labels.
- Treated the new asset click/conversion columns as subject to the same combination bias as the old labels, per Google's own "directional" note and Geddes' reasoning.

## Canon strength: Strong
Geddes/Adalysis (three pieces, 2022–2026, with account examples), Vallaeys/Optmyzr (2021, 2022), plus the platform-change record; Google docs for current behaviour.

## Invalidated by
Google exposing per-combination clicks and conversions (which would remove the main blind spot), or removing asset-level statistics again.
