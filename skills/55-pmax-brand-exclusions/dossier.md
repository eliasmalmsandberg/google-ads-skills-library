## Dissenting view
Kirk Williams (2022, 2026) argues against reflexive exclusion: brand is mid-funnel for multi-retailer brands, and he has seen total brand sales fall after exclusion. Nils Rooijmans (2022) and the smec/Rhodes tooling assume the opposite default: brand inflates PMax and must be separated for a clean read. The disagreement is about what the brand query is (navigational vs comparison) and whether the loss of a clean PMax read matters more than the risk of losing brand volume in the handover.

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- Brand exclusions in PMax: campaign setting applying an account-level brand list. Announced "coming soon" Feb 2023; rolled out to all advertisers after the 2023-06-29 announcement (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13729701). Apply to Search, Shopping and YouTube search inventory only (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14505308); Display/Video/Discover/Gmail unaffected.
- Matching uses the newest broad match: misspellings, variants and foreign-language brand searches are covered without listing them; distinct names (e.g. "Google", "YouTube", "Alphabet") must each be listed (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13721847).
- Checkbox "Allow Shopping ads on searches that mention excluded brands" keeps Shopping ads serving on excluded-brand queries.
- Brands missing from Google's library can be requested; the brand is usable immediately by the requester.
- Google's stated caveat: exclusions reduce reach, "apply only where needed", monitor for missed conversions.
- Search-campaign brand exclusions moved into the AI Max panel on 2025-05-27; Google states this does not affect PMax.
- Account-level negative keywords apply to all Search and Shopping inventory including PMax; campaign-level negatives in PMax exist (Search & Shopping inventory only) (https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15726455).

## Judgment calls
- Threshold for acting: canon gives no single number. PPC.io's distribution (median 14%, top third >20%) and Pooler's 30%-of-conversions flag bracket the range. Skill uses 20% of PMax clicks or 30% of PMax conversions as the "act" line, below 10% as "leave it", and the band between as "test".
- Shopping checkbox: canon is silent. Skill follows Kirk's prerequisite (a brand Standard Shopping campaign must exist before brand Shopping traffic is cut from PMax).
- PPC.io and smec added as Tier 2 for this topic; Haus figures are secondhand and flagged as such.

## Canon strength: Strong
