## What the dossier found
- One Tier 1 line: Navah Hopkins (SEJ, 2025) says to avoid dynamic keyword insertion in competitor campaigns and keep competitor names out of ad copy. No method, no data.
- Google docs (fetched 2026-10-13): insertion uses the triggering ad-group keyword, not the search term; `{KeyWord:fallback}` syntax with the fallback used when no keyword fits or the keyword is too long; same behaviour across match types; policy compliance is the advertiser's responsibility, especially with Google-recommended keywords. Google's two help pages do not document the capitalisation codes (keyword / Keyword / KeyWord / KEYWord / KEYWORD) or any RSA-specific rule, so the commonly cited case table could not be verified from Google in this build.
- Non-canon material (PPC Hero, 2007–2016; recorded, not added to Tier 2): the familiar warnings — DKI with broad match inserts irrelevant text, misspelled keywords get inserted, single-keyword headlines look spammy, and needing DKI to cover every keyword signals an over-stuffed ad group — plus one single-account headline test reporting +36% impression share and +57% CVR without baselines or significance. None passes the inclusion rule (method + evidence + public disagreement with Google).
- Adalysis' RSA handbook, PPC Mastery's public material and Optmyzr's reachable posts contain nothing on DKI. Search Engine Land could not be fetched (rate-limited) during this build.
