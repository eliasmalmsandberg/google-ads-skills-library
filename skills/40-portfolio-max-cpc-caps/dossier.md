## Dissenting view
Google (Target CPA help page) and Hopkins' study both argue against caps as a default: Google says caps "restrict automatic bid optimization"; Hopkins finds the effect inconsistent and that targets/constraints hurt more often than help. KlientBoost and Bharadwaj argue caps are the main reason to use a portfolio at all. The disagreement is about *when*: whether a cap is a standing control (KlientBoost) or an exception for a diagnosed CPC-spike pattern (Rooijmans, Hopkins). Vallaeys adds a timing dissent: don't add caps as a reflex to the Aug 2026 change.

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- [Google docs] Bid limits (max and min CPC) exist only on portfolio strategies, not standard campaign-level tCPA/tROAS; they apply to Search Network auctions only; not available for display-only campaigns; Google states setting them "isn't recommended". Min bid can still be undercut by smart pricing. (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6268632, fetched 2026-10-13.)
- [Google docs / PPC News Feed 2025-10-29] Bid limits were added to Max Conversion Value portfolio strategies in Oct 2025, only on newly created portfolios and only after a tROAS is set.
- [Google docs / SEJ 2020-03-10] Portfolios have supported Max Conversions and Max Conversion Value since March 2020.
- [Google docs via Vallaeys/TechWyse] 17 Aug 2026 (rollout complete 27 Aug): budget-limited tCPA/tROAS campaigns no longer have bids suppressed to ration budget; they now bid toward the stated target. Google advises against adding bid limits because of this change. Bid Target Adjustment Tool available since 6 Jul 2026.
- [Google docs] Enhanced CPC retired March 2025 (per Hopkins study).

## Baseline check
Read `google-ads-bidding` (portfolio section, bid adjustments) and `google-ads-budget-management` (Smart Bidding × budget). Baseline says portfolios "consolidate data" and mentions "spend caps" in passing; it does not know caps are portfolio-only, gives no cap-setting rule, no study evidence on when caps help, and predates the Aug 2026 budget-limited change which is the main reason the topic is live now. This skill adds: the cap-sizing rules (25% above avg CPC vs ≤10% of daily budget), the strategy-specific evidence (tROAS/tIS benefit, tCPA mixed), the single-campaign-portfolio trick, and the post-Aug-2026 decision rule.

## Judgment calls
- KlientBoost and Mike Ryan added as topic-scoped Tier 2 (both publish method/data and depart from Google). Their numbers are flagged as uncontrolled.
- Where the two cap-sizing rules conflict (25% above avg CPC vs ≤10% of daily budget), the skill takes the lower of the two as the starting cap because Hopkins has the cross-account evidence and the KlientBoost figure is one agency's practice.
- Treated the Aug 2026 change as the primary trigger for this skill in 2026: the "overpaying on clicks" symptom in the index row is now most often this.

## Canon strength: Strong
3 Tier 1 sources (Hopkins with cross-account evidence; Rooijmans; Vallaeys) plus two added Tier 2 with account data.

## Invalidated by
Google making bid limits available on standard (non-portfolio) strategies, removing them from portfolios, extending them beyond Search auctions, or reversing the Aug 2026 budget-limited bidding change.
