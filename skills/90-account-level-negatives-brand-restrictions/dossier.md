## Dissenting view
Rooijmans (2025) argues *against* account-level negatives as the PMax tool because they hit every campaign, preferring a shared list scoped to PMax. Google's own PMax guidance (Help) argues against negatives at all in PMax — "a highly restrictive control" — and pushes brand exclusions. Vallaeys (2025) shows brand exclusions leak, and routes the fix through positive keywords in Search rather than more exclusion. The disagreement is about *scope*: whether an account-wide block is the right instrument for terms that are genuinely wrong for the whole account (jobs, free, porn, competitor-support) versus terms that are wrong for one campaign type only (brand in PMax).

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- [Google docs] Account-level negative keywords: one list under Admin → Account settings → Negative keywords; applies to search and shopping inventory of Search, Performance Max, App, Shopping, Smart and Local campaigns; **limit 1,000 per account**; broad/phrase/exact set per keyword; negatives do not match close variants ("flowers" broad blocks "red flowers" not "red flower"). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11396330
- [Google docs] Negative keywords generally: broad is the default negative match type; no close-variant matching, so plurals and synonyms must be added by hand; misspellings and casing are handled. Display/Video consider at most 1,000 account-level negatives. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972
- [Google docs] PMax negatives: campaign-level negatives now self-serve under Campaigns → Keywords → Negative keywords with an impact estimate; existing lists can be attached; account-level negatives apply automatically to PMax search and shopping inventory. Google calls negatives in PMax "a highly restrictive control" and recommends brand exclusions instead for brand terms. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15726455 and /16668865
- [Google docs] Brand exclusions: block ads on searches for a brand name, common misspellings and related subsidiaries; cover Search and Shopping ads in PMax (Shopping can be allowed to keep serving); available on new or existing PMax and Search campaigns; Google calls them "the most effective way to control how your ads appear on searches for your brand" and recommends them when brand is run in a separate campaign. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16669487
- [Google docs] Final URL expansion remains on by default in PMax (relevant because it widens the query set the negatives have to police). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14337539
- Timeline [Optmyzr summaries]: account-level negatives rep-only until at least April 2023, then self-serve; PMax brand exclusions announced Feb 2023; PMax campaign-level negatives beta in early 2025, now GA per Google Help.

## Baseline check
Read `google-ads-negative-keywords` (shared-list architecture, starter universal list, "account (via shared list)" level — it predates native account-level negatives and never mentions the 1,000 cap, PMax coverage, brand exclusions or close-variant non-matching) and `google-ads-account-audit` (no account-level coverage). This skill adds the three-instrument split (account-level list / PMax-scoped list / brand exclusions), the cap-budgeting rule, the brand-leak fix via positive exact keywords, and the measurement via brand-share reporting.

## Judgment calls
- Position: account-level list is reserved for terms wrong for *every* campaign in the account; brand terms never go there (it would block the brand campaign). Brand restriction on PMax = brand exclusions + brand-campaign exact keywords incl. misspellings (Vallaeys), with a PMax-scoped negative list as the backstop (Rooijmans).
- Cap budgeting (reserve ~200 of 1,000 for emergency blocks) is an Epiminds operating rule, flagged as such.
- Phrase as default for account-level entries (not Google's broad default) is taken from the baseline's convention and the close-variant behaviour in Google docs — flagged as Epiminds' choice since no canon source states it for account-level specifically.
- Hopkins 2026 post used only to corroborate Google docs because its dates are inconsistent.

## Canon strength: Adequate
Vallaeys (2025) brings cross-account evidence; Rooijmans (2025) and Rhodes (script) bring method; the rest is Google docs. No practitioner test of account-level negatives' effect on performance exists in the canon.

## Invalidated by
The 1,000-keyword account cap changing; account-level negatives gaining campaign-type scoping; brand exclusions extending to close variants reliably (per a new Optmyzr/Adalysis study); PMax negatives moving to asset-group level.
