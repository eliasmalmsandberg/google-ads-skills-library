# Dossier — 88 Standard off-list (partners, display expansion, URL expansion, auto-apply)

6. **Source** Navah Hopkins, Optmyzr, "Does Optimization Score affect Google Ads performance?", https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/does-optimization-score-affect-google-ads-performance/ · **Tier** 1 · **Date** 2024-08-23 · **Claim** 17,380 accounts (≥90 days, ≥$500/mo, ≤$1M/mo). 19% of accounts reached OptiScore 90+ without accepting any recommendation; only 5.5% of accounts accepted recommendations at all; the most-accepted ones were hygiene fixes (conflicting negatives, missing assets). ## Dissenting view
Geddes (2023) disagrees with the blanket "partners off" position: he has accounts where partner CPA beat Google Search, and says the network should be judged per campaign on CPA/ROAS rather than blocked by default — except for small, budget-limited accounts. Rooijmans' own PS (2026) concedes this for mature campaigns. The disagreement is about *sequence*: off-then-earn-it-back (Rooijmans) vs on-then-cut-if-worse (Geddes). On Display Expansion, Seer's US result (+32% conversions at +51% CPL) shows it is not always pure waste; the disagreement is whether a 50%+ CPL penalty is ever acceptable for incremental volume. On AAR, Hopkins (2024) shows the accounts that accepted recommendations and reached 90+ performed best — but it was 333 accounts, and the accepted items were hygiene.

## What the platform currently does [Google docs]
- [Google docs] Search partners are on by default for Search campaigns; Google does not name partner sites in standard reporting (PMax excepted); partner CTR does not affect Quality Score; Google itself warns partner clicks on product-listing sites "may not reflect highly targeted traffic". Opt out per campaign under Networks. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722047
- [Google docs] Display Expansion on Search: Google claims it uses "unspent Search budgets at similar cost per conversion as Search", serves only when it predicts unused budget, and stops if Search CPA cannot be matched; not available with shared budgets or experiments. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7193800 — Industry reporting (PPC News Feed, 2025-06-19) says the box stopped being pre-ticked for new Search campaigns in June 2025 after being pre-ticked in Aug 2024.
- [Google docs] Final URL expansion is **on by default** in PMax; it replaces your Final URL with another page on the domain based on query intent and generates dynamic headlines. Off = traffic goes only to asset-group URLs / page feed. URL exclusions (exact or "URL contains") exist only while expansion is on. Google recommends on "for broad reach". Not supported for store-goal-only campaigns. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14337539 and /16672777. Text customization (formerly automatically created assets) is a separate toggle.
- [Google docs] Auto-apply recommendations: account-level only (not MCC); categories include optimized ad rotation, bid target changes, broad match / keyword additions, "expand reach through Search partners or Display", targeting expansion, removal of redundant/non-serving/conflicting keywords, conversion tracking upgrades. Can be turned on/off at any time; dismissed items visible in Manage/History. "Add RSAs" removed from AAR on 2026-01-26. Auto-apply does not raise budgets. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10279006
- [Google docs, via PPC News Feed 2026-04-30] PMax "Display controls" now expose Search Partner Network and Google Display Network opt-outs.

## Judgment calls
- Four settings treated as one "off-list" because the canon (Rooijmans 2026) treats them as one takeover action, and because AAR is the mechanism that silently re-enables the other three.
- Final URL expansion has the thinnest practitioner evidence: no Tier 1 source gives a direct test. The rule is built from Google's own description (replaces your URL, generates headlines), Vallaeys' overlap study (keywordless PMax reach is where Search overlap comes from), and Ryan/smec's DSA analogy. Flagged in the skill as the weakest rule.
- Seer added as Tier 2 for Display Expansion only.
- Menachem Ani excluded from claims (no evidence); used only to note that the "keep optimized ad rotation on" position exists.
- Post-review: the ≥ 30 conversions/30 days earn-back gate, the 90-day re-check cadence and the < $500/month tiny-account cut-off are Epiminds operating rules with no canon source (Rooijmans 2026 says "mature" without a number; the $500 floor is borrowed from Hopkins 2024's study inclusion criterion). The two PMax/Display-Expansion platform-change claims are attributed to PPC News Feed (2025-06-19, citing Jyll Saskin Gales; 2026-04-30, citing Dekker/Spin/Schwartz) — industry reporting, not Google docs, and labelled as such.

## Canon strength: Strong
Geddes (2023, numbers), Hopkins (2024, 17,380 accounts), Vallaeys (2023 anecdote; 2025 503-account study), Rooijmans ×3 (method, script), Seer (test with numbers).

## Invalidated by
Google defaulting Search partners or Display Expansion to off; Google exposing partner-site reporting or exclusions for Search; Final URL expansion defaulting to off or gaining a keyword-level control; AAR being removed or made MCC-level.
